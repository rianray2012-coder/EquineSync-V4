# W1-RF01 Historical Identity Continuity Plan

- Preserve immutable original user/actor identifiers on audit and domain records.
- Add aliases and successor links instead of rewriting historical authorship.
- Record merge/split proposals as reviewable cases with provenance, confidence, and before/after access deltas.
- Never merge solely by name, email, phone, barn, horse, or payment identity.
- Retain ended memberships and relationships with effective and termination dates.
- Recalculate live permissions after account reactivation; do not automatically restore old access.
- Separate retention, stewardship, authorship, and direct application access.
- Support lawful erasure through minimization/tombstoning without falsifying audit lineage.
- Quarantine disputed, deceased, estate, minor, professional, and international cases for governed handling.
- Require rollback eligibility before any future bulk mapping or migration.
