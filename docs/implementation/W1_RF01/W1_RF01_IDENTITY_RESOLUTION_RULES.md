# W1-RF01 Identity Resolution Rules

1. Stable internal IDs outrank mutable contact fields.
2. Email, name, phone, barn, horse, payment method, and provider identity are evidence, never sufficient automatic identity proof.
3. Exact verified account links may propose a match but still require provenance and conflict checks.
4. Multiple accounts may represent one person until a governed merge is approved; do not collapse silently.
5. One account must not silently control multiple actors.
6. Merge retains aliases, original IDs, authorship, audit lineage, and reversible mapping.
7. Split restores separate actors without rewriting historical evidence.
8. Guardian/minor, deceased/estate, disputed, professional, and international cases require specialist review.
9. Provider identities require explicit practice/person/grant separation.
10. Every decision records confidence, evidence, reviewer, timestamp, reason, before/after access, and rollback eligibility.
