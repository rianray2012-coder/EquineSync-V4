## Included baseline

The immutable Governance V1.0 baseline includes:

1. `docs/canon/`, including adopted sources, adoption records, lock records, lock certificates, post-lock protocols, registries, founder decisions, requirement traceability, dependency and authority evidence, retained-P2 records, provenance, and supersession evidence.
2. `docs/implementation/`, excluding operating-system metadata, as the adopted planning Atlas and authorized implementation-governance record set. Inclusion does not lock the Implementation Atlas where its own lifecycle says it remains adopted but not locked.
3. `docs/governance_v1_0/`, containing the aggregate baseline scope, manifest, validation, lock, and change-control evidence.
4. `.github/CODEOWNERS`, containing review ownership for constitutional and implementation-governance paths.

## Exclusions

Application source, database state, runtime configuration, credentials, secrets, local environment files, caches, temporary renders, customer data, provider configuration, deployment state, and unrelated working-tree changes are excluded.

Historical evidence packages may reference files outside this aggregate baseline. Such references remain historical provenance and do not silently expand the current baseline scope.

## Immutable boundary

The aggregate baseline manifest is the current-state checksum authority for this global lock. Earlier manifests remain immutable point-in-time evidence for their original lifecycle events. They are not rewritten or reclassified as current-state aggregate manifests.
