## Cross-Canon Review

The candidate explicitly preserves:

- Relationship Model ownership of relationship semantics;
- Record Stewardship ownership of records, retention, transfer, correction,
  minimization, consent evidence, and disposition;
- Claims/Disputes ownership of contested-claim procedure and temporary
  restrictions;
- Permission ownership of final authorization and field projection;
- domain/lifecycle ownership of domain behavior;
- external-provider status as adapter/evidence, never authority.

No direct contradiction with the available locked canons was found. The named
Master Claims, Disputes, and Authority Model was not available for file-level
comparison, and both Stewardship v2.0 and v2.1 exist in Downloads. Those are
founder lock-review evidence items, not permission to infer or rewrite either
canon.
