# Domain Owner and Steward Registry

**Version:** 1.0 controlled candidate
**Status:** Founder review pending

| Domain | Constitutional owner | Operational steward | Evidence steward | Authority boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product vision | Founder/Product Governance | Product leadership | Canon governance | Highest product-purpose authority |
| Ecosystem | Ecosystem canon owner | Product architecture | Canon governance | Principal/entity/event placement |
| Relationships | Relationship canon owner | Domain services under future RFs | Relationship/audit steward | Relationship facts never grant fields directly |
| Records/retention | Stewardship canon owner | Domain record stewards | Records/legal-hold steward | Authorship, retention, correction, erasure, export |
| Claims/disputes | Claims canon owner | Authorized claims reviewers | Claims/evidence steward | Neutral claims; no EquineSync legal adjudication |
| Permissions | Permission canon owner | Security/authorization engineering | Access audit steward | Final action and projection authority |
| Horse transfer | Horse Transfer Policy/RF31 after adoption | Future RF31 transfer operations | Passport/transfer evidence steward | Coordinates but does not own facility, finance, Calendar, or legal outcomes |
| Identity/actors | Locked Identity V2.0 canon owner | Identity/security operations | Identity audit steward | Identity, account, actor, principal, representation identity and attribution; no permission or relationship authority |
| Agreements/consent | Agreement canon owner after adoption | Agreement operations | Agreement/evidence steward | Exact version, parties, capacity, consent, obligation effects |
| Audit/evidence | Audit canon owner after adoption | Security/platform evidence operations | Audit evidence steward | Material event/evidence integrity and production |
| Communications/notice | Communications canon owner after adoption | Communications operations | Communication evidence steward | Eligibility, classification, delivery/receipt evidence, escalation |
| Platform reliability/release | Platform Operations canon owner after adoption | SRE/release engineering | Operational evidence steward | Environments, changes, releases, incidents, recovery |
| External adapters | Locked External Architecture canon owner | Integration engineering/vendor operations | Adapter evidence steward | Immutable constitutional reference; vendors never create domain authority; implementation remains separately gated |
| Financial truth | Locked Financial Truth V2.1 canon owner | Finance operations and future RF32/financial-rails/accounting-integration owners | Financial/audit steward | Money objects, roles, obligations, settlement, payout, reconciliation, accounting projection, continuity and segregation; no runtime authority from canon lock |
| Record governance matrix | Stewardship owner | Canon governance | Governance evidence steward | Controlled instrument, not constitutional authority |

Named individuals, legal entities, on-call assignments, and service accounts require separate approved operational registries. This document assigns domain responsibility classes only.
