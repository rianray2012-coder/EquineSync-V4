# Equine Health Terminology and Definition Reconciliation

| Term | Specialized meaning | Owning canon | Reconciliation rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Welfare observation | Attributable non-diagnostic observation about condition or environment | Health | Must preserve context/confidence and not become diagnosis |
| Routine care record | Operational husbandry or preventive-care record | Health/Operations | Completion does not prove clinical effect |
| Medical record | Health record with clinical or treatment relevance | Health/Stewardship | Sensitivity and provenance mandatory |
| Clinical judgment | Professional interpretation by an accountable qualified actor | Health | Distinct from measurement and AI output |
| Diagnosis | Professional clinical conclusion | Health/professional authority | Never inferred from upload, payment, or model output |
| Treatment instruction | Attributable authorized care direction | Health | Source revision must govern tasks |
| Medication order | Authorized instruction for medication use | Health | Distinct from schedule and administration |
| Administration record | Evidence of actual or attempted dose event | Health/Audit | Missed/refused/late/unknown remain distinct |
| Critical alert | Qualified safety information requiring conspicuous projection | Health/Permission | Does not expose full history |
| Private provider note | Source-retained deliberative or private professional note | Health/Stewardship | Not automatically transferable |
| Health continuity package | Approved minimum-necessary transfer projection | Transfer/Health | Not full record export |
| Quarantine | Scoped movement/interaction restriction | Health/Facility | Does not itself expose diagnosis |
| Clinical decision support | Non-authoritative source-grounded assistance | AI/Health | Requires human professional review |
| Medical-sensitive status | Status implying illness, injury, treatment restriction, or recovery | Health/Permission | Redact when medical permission is false |
| Emergency directive | Time-sensitive professional or policy-backed care direction | Health/Claims | Temporary and audited, not durable authority |
