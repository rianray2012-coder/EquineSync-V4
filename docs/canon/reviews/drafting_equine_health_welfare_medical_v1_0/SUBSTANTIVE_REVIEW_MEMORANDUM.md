## Executive Finding

The original draft correctly protected medical-sensitive fields and separated clinical authority from account, payment, and provider presence. It required deeper semantics for record classes, professional boundaries, medication events, controlled treatments, welfare observations, emergency referral and end of life, clinical decision support, offline conflicts, device provenance, and private-note continuity.

The revised draft incorporates noncontroversial protections while preserving fourteen material founder choices. No P0 defect was found. Fourteen P1 adoption blockers and six P2 governance follow-ups remain.

## Existing Founder Direction Incorporated

- the horse remains a durable canonical identity across transfer and account change;
- clinical, owner-reported, staff-observed, imported, inferred, and projected information remain distinct;
- Permission makes final medical access and field-projection decisions;
- backend redaction is primary and frontend hiding is defense in depth;
- Care Circle, payment, possession, facility membership, or provider account does not create medical authority;
- disputes preserve neutral competing evidence and do not make EquineSync a veterinary board or legal adjudicator;
- treatment authority and financial responsibility remain separate;
- emergency welfare may justify narrow temporary action, not durable authority;
- external providers are adapters and cannot overwrite canonical authority;
- AI cannot diagnose, prescribe, select treatment, determine lameness, or execute care;
- offline state must fail closed for high-risk medication and authority conflicts.
