# Safeguarding V1.2 AI Reconciliation

AI remains assistive only. It may organize intake, detect duplicates, translate, prompt for missing information, surface urgent language for authorized human review, build timelines, route, and redact. It may not determine abuse, danger, credibility, guilt, reporting duty, discipline, guardian fitness, closure, or predictive safeguarding profiles. Protected data may not train general or shared models. RF30 and controlling AI governance remain unchanged; no AI behavior is authorized.
