# Safeguarding V1.2 Equine Health Reconciliation

The candidate permits immediate, narrowly scoped emergency communication for human or horse safety but does not grant clinical authority, medication authority, veterinary decision authority, or emergency treatment disposition to a minor, guardian, safeguarding actor, or platform workflow. Equine-health truth and professional decisions remain with their governing domain and qualified humans. No P0 or P1 conflict was identified.
