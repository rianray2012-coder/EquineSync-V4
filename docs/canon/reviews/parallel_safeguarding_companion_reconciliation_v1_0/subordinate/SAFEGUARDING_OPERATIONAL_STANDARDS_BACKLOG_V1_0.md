# Safeguarding Operational Standards Backlog V1.0

## 112. Required Metric Families

A later implementation should produce privacy-protective metrics for:

- blocked one-to-one adult-minor messaging attempts;
- guardian-inclusion failures;
- protected thread recipient changes;
- safeguarding intake volume and age;
- emergency escalations;
- protective restriction duration and review;
- adult eligibility expiration and revocation;
- pickup and transportation denial events;
- public-media withdrawal propagation;
- unauthorized access attempts;
- reporter-identity exposure incidents;
- offline revocation completion;
- appeal and correction timing;
- training and acknowledgment status;
- vendor and plugin access attempts;
- guardian delivery failures and no-guardian communication blocks;
- external-reporting workflow age and unresolved status;
- case conflict, recusal, and reassignment events;
- exceptional access and bulk export attempts;
- safety-plan age, scope, and review completion;
- case separation and linked-case disclosure failures.

## 113. Metric Misuse Prohibited

Metrics may not be used to:

- pressure reporters not to submit concerns;
- reward low report counts;
- penalize organizations merely for recording concerns;
- force premature closure;
- rank minors or reporters;
- infer guilt from report volume;
- create public safety scores without separate authority;
- measure staff performance by low report counts or short closure times;
- infer that an organization is safe because few concerns were recorded.
