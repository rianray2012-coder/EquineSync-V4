# Safeguarding Parallel Text Section Disposition Matrix

## Executive Disposition

The V1.1 text is substantively strong and contains many protections that should survive reconciliation. It should not be merged as one monolithic constitutional document. Constitutional rules, readiness gates, test cases, registers, and operational standards need separate lifecycles.

## Constitutional Material to Retain or Merge

- Parts I-V: ownership, definitions, age, autonomy, guardian authority, and accounts.
- Parts VII-XVII: protected intake, emergency controls, adult eligibility, physical interaction, horse-care limits, privacy, AI, records, fairness, adulthood transition, offline boundaries, and responsibilities.
- Parts XXII-XXIII: prohibited conduct, non-waivable floors, exceptions, and founder-reserved authority.
- The stronger controls for Safety Plans, guardian delivery failure, no-guardian states, case conflict and recusal, responding-person notice, eligibility revalidation, lodging/private spaces, gifts/coercion, transport, non-retaliation, AI profiling, shared-model training, bulk access, and chain of custody.

## Material Requiring Founder Decision

- Part VI's absolute adult/minor ordinary electronic communication prohibition.
- The combined Protected Participant definition in Part II.
- Approval and controlling-status assertions in document control and Appendix A.

## Material to Move

- Part XVIII to the Implementation Readiness Standard and Operational Backlog.
- Part XIX to the Implementation Readiness Standard.
- Part XX and Appendix D to the Constitutional Test Catalog.
- Part XXI to readiness and assurance standards.
- Appendices A-B to the Register and Evidence Inventory.
- Appendix C to the Operational Standards Backlog.

The complete section-by-section classification is recorded in `SAFEGUARDING_PARALLEL_TEXT_SECTION_DISPOSITION_MATRIX.csv`. No section was silently discarded.
