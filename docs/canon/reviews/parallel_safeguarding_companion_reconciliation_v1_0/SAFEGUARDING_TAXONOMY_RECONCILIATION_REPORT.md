# Safeguarding Taxonomy Reconciliation Report

## Finding

The parallel definition of `Protected Participant` improperly combines enduring or account-relevant status with case confidentiality and temporary restrictions. That combination could create stigmatizing durable labels, overbroad permission effects, and accidental disclosure that a person is an alleged victim, reporter, witness, or restricted subject.

## Recommended Canonical Separation

### Identity and Account Status

- minor under applicable jurisdiction;
- adult;
- account managed or assisted under independently evidenced authority;
- account independently controlled;
- identity or age unverified.

Identity status does not establish capacity, guardianship, case involvement, or permission.

### Decision-Specific Capacity and Assistance Context

- supported decision-making arrangement;
- guardianship or conservatorship authority;
- decision-specific capacity limitation;
- temporary assistance;
- restored or changed autonomy.

These are scoped, temporal relationship and authority facts, not global person labels.

### Safeguarding Case Role

- person reporting;
- alleged affected person;
- witness;
- responding person;
- confidential source;
- person receiving protective support.

Case roles are visible only within authorized case contexts and do not become general profile attributes.

### Protective Handling State

- ordinary safeguards;
- heightened confidentiality;
- temporary safety plan;
- restricted contact;
- do-not-notify condition;
- prohibited contact;
- sealed or specially routed record.

Protective handling controls capabilities and projection; it does not determine guilt, capacity, identity, or legal status.

## Recommended Use of `Protected Participant`

Retain the phrase only as a policy umbrella meaning: a person for whom a specific context requires heightened safeguarding controls. It must be computed from authorized evidence for a stated purpose, never exposed as a broad profile badge, and never independently grant or deny access.

## Migration and Permission Rule

No existing field may be promoted into these states without provenance, scope, effective dates, access-delta review, and quarantine for ambiguity. Permission remains the final authorization and field-projection authority.
