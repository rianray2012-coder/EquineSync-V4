# Identity, Account, and Actor V2.0 Required Corrections and Expansions

## Required corrections before controlled adoption: completed

### IA-C01: Candidate authority posture

Replace self-executing “controlling,” “adopts,” and lower-order supremacy language with candidate-state text. Define scoped authority: Identity governs durable identity, account, actor/principal, authentication context, and attribution; it does not override Relationship, Permission, Claims, Stewardship, Agreement, or domain canons.

### IA-C02: Historical provenance

State that Version 1 is unavailable, complete predecessor preservation cannot be verified, and the Historical Provenance Exception Ledger governs any later recovery.

### IA-C03: Current cross-canon references

Use current versioned paths for Relationship, Stewardship V2.1, Claims V2.0, Permission, and locked External Architecture V2.0. Label Audit, Agreement, Communications, Financial Truth, Platform Operations, and any future Security canon by current state. Do not cite nonexistent files as active authority.

### IA-C04: Implementation-neutral security and federation

Convert protocols, providers, authentication methods, AAL labels, and operational control examples into explicitly illustrative, replaceable, non-authorizing examples. Constitutional requirements should describe capabilities and invariants; External Architecture and future implementation gates select mechanisms.

### IA-C05: Domain ownership matrix

Add an explicit matrix separating:

- Identity: durable identity, accounts, actor/principal context, authentication context, attribution references;
- Relationship: membership, representation, delegation, guardian/fiduciary and organization relationships;
- Permission: action/field authorization;
- Claims: dispute procedure and restrictions;
- Agreement: party capacity, consent, and effect;
- Stewardship: identity-linked record handling;
- Audit: audit event/evidence truth;
- External Architecture: provider/federation adapter boundaries;
- Communications/Financial/domain canons: their own role, routing, and lifecycle truth.

## Recommended expansions before lock

### IA-E01: Constitutional version and amendment contract

Define model version, effective date, supersession evidence, compatibility review, migration non-authority, and amendment lifecycle.

### IA-E02: High-risk identity decision governance

Require human review, evidence thresholds, appeal, non-discrimination, privacy minimization, and reversible/quarantined handling for merges, guardian/fiduciary authority, deceased identity, professional verification, and global identity linking.

### IA-E03: Identity visibility classification

Define whether the existence of an identity, alias, guardian, fiduciary, professional credential, disputed link, or historical name is public, relational, confidential, restricted, or existence-protected. Visibility remains a Permission input, not an Identity grant.

### IA-E04: Controlled registry maturity

Classify the proposed registries as future controlled artifacts. None may be assumed populated or implementation-ready through constitutional adoption.
