# Identity, Account, and Actor V2.0 Domain Ownership Boundary Matrix

| Concept | Constitutional owner | Identity-owned element | Prohibited Identity inference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Person/organization/system identity | Identity | Durable reference and history | Identity does not establish relationships or permissions |
| Account/credential/session/device | Identity for constitutional association/context; future Security/Platform for operations | Account association and authentication context | Account control is not legal identity or authority |
| Membership | Relationship/Permission | Endpoint and attribution reference | Membership is not created by identity linkage |
| Role assignment | Permission/Relationship | Subject and assigning-actor reference | Role existence does not grant universal authority |
| Representation/delegation/guardian/fiduciary | Relationship; Agreement/Claims where applicable | Parties and actor chain | Verification does not prove legal capacity or current authority |
| Claims/disputes | Claims | Claimant, subject, verifier, source | Identity does not decide truth or appeal |
| Agreements/consent | Agreement | Party, signer, representative references | Identity does not establish assent, capacity, formation, or effect |
| Audit evidence | Audit | Stable actor attribution | Identity does not own audit records or sufficiency |
| Communications/notices | Communication | Sender/recipient references | Identity does not determine eligibility, notice effect, or delivery truth |
| Financial roles/states | Financial Truth | Actor/payor/payee/holder references | Identity does not create liability, balance, settlement, or ownership |
| Records | Stewardship | Subject/author/custodian/actor references | Identity does not determine retention, transfer, export, or disposition |
| External identity provider/federation | External Architecture | Binding to canonical identity/account reference | Provider assertion does not become canonical/legal authority |

This matrix is a constitutional ownership rule, not a schema or implementation design.
