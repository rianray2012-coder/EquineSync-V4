# Identity, Account, and Actor V2.0 Adoption Protocol and Provider Neutrality Verification

Contextual review covered OAuth/OpenID concepts, SAML, OIDC, SCIM, MFA, social and enterprise identity providers, biometrics, verification services, directory platforms, credential providers, passkeys, one-time codes and AAL labels.

Result: `PASS`.

- Every example is controlled by section 4.11 as illustrative, replaceable and non-authorizing.
- No provider, protocol, vendor, platform, assurance label, library, framework or technology has preferred, default, mandatory, approved or selected constitutional status.
- Capability outcomes are constitutional; mechanism selection remains with locked External Architecture and later founder-governed implementation.
- No credential issuance, provider binding, federation, MFA, SSO or runtime activation is authorized.
