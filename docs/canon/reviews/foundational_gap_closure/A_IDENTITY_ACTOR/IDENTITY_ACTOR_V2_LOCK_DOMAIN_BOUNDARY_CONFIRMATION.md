# Identity V2.0 Lock Domain-Boundary Confirmation

| Identity governs | Adjacent authority retained elsewhere |
| --- | --- |
| Canonical identity truth, actors, account association, identity continuity, attribution, representation identity, system/automated actor identity, external identity evidence boundaries | Relationship existence; permissions and authorization outcomes; claims truth; agreement formation and legal capacity; financial responsibility; communication sufficiency; retention/disposal; audit evidentiary sufficiency |

The Canon Index, dependency registry, adoption report, and adopted body preserve this division. No post-adoption record transfers adjacent-domain authority into Identity or transfers Identity authority elsewhere.
