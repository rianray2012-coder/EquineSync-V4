## Constitutional strengths

- Durable person identity independent of accounts, credentials, roles, and organizations.
- Explicit actor, principal, represented principal, and complete attribution chains.
- Strong minor/guardian, estate, fiduciary, deceased-person, support, emergency, AI, service-account, and cross-tenant protections.
- Identity uncertainty, duplicate resolution, historical authorship, and account-control disputes remain visible and auditable.
- External identity providers authenticate but do not define EquineSync authority.
- Account closure, transfer, death, and organizational change do not erase identity or attribution.
