## 2. Executive Summary

The EquineSync application shell shall present one coherent visual system across web, iOS, Android, and supported responsive surfaces. The system shall express quiet luxury, professional equestrian heritage, modern SaaS precision, warmth, and operational clarity without allowing decorative brand elements to interfere with care work, search, navigation, safety, accessibility, or authorization.

The approved visual hierarchy is:

1. **Full EquineSync logo:** formal brand identification where the name must be read.
2. **Circuit-horse mark:** primary product mark and primary production app icon.
3. **9D Circle Monogram:** secondary compact mark and approved optional alternate app icon.
4. **9C Speed Lines Mark:** ultra-small functional favicon and constrained system mark.
5. **EquineSync Stead:** mascot, guidance, personality, and controlled product delight.

The approved typography hierarchy is:

1. **Bright Demo:** expressive brand headlines and hero statements.
2. **Bodoni Moda:** “In Sync.” and premium signature moments.
3. **Lora:** body copy, editorial content, documentation, and spacious product copy.
4. **Operational utility typeface:** not yet Founder-approved and therefore recorded as an open material decision.

The EquineSync Stead shall remain distinct from the formal corporate and product marks. It may create warmth and discovery in low-risk workflows, but it shall not appear in solemn, emergency, safeguarding, legal, financial-dispute, security, privacy-incident, or evidentiary contexts.

## 3. Purpose, Outcomes, and Success Measures

### 3.1 Purpose

This section establishes the implementation contract for how EquineSync appears, identifies itself, and uses branded visual elements within the application shell.

### 3.2 Required outcomes

The design shall ensure that:

1. users recognize EquineSync across platforms and size constraints;
2. formal brand identity remains consistent without forcing one mark into every size;
3. typography supports both premium brand expression and operational readability;
4. the shell remains usable in outdoor glare, on mobile devices, one-handed, and under poor connectivity;

6. icons, color, typography, and motion never become a substitute for authorization or text;
7. the EquineSync Stead adds controlled warmth without trivializing serious workflows;

9. app icons and alternate icons do not imply plan, role, facility, authority, or account state; and

## 6. Definitions and Controlled Vocabulary

- **Application Shell:** Persistent product frame containing navigation, context, search entry points, account controls, status surfaces, and shared interaction patterns.
- **Formal Brand Identity:** Full EquineSync logo and approved product marks used to identify the company or platform.
- **Circuit-Horse Mark:** Approved horse silhouette incorporating circuit and motion elements.
- **9D Circle Monogram:** Compact “E-S” monogram within a circle, approved as a secondary compact mark and optional alternate app icon.
- **9C Speed Lines Mark:** Ultra-minimal speed-line mark forming an “E”-like structure, approved for the smallest constrained surfaces.
- **EquineSync Stead:** Mascot and product-delight character. It is not the formal logo and does not represent authority, safety status, or record truth.
- **Serious Workflow:** A workflow involving injury, emergency care, safeguarding, abuse or neglect, medication error, payment dispute, legal notice, security incident, privacy incident, outage, evidence preservation, suspension, evacuation, or another high-consequence condition.
- **Brand Display Typography:** Bright Demo and Bodoni Moda used only for approved high-impact brand moments.
- **Reading Typography:** Lora used for body, editorial, educational, documentation, and sufficiently spacious product copy.
- **Operational Utility Typeface:** A compact, highly legible typeface used for navigation, controls, tables, scheduling, search, badges, timestamps, and dense interfaces. Founder selection remains open.
- **Optical Simplification:** Approved reduction of detail for small-size clarity without changing the identity or creating a new mark.
- **Alternate App Icon:** User-selectable cosmetic icon that does not change product functionality, plan, authority, facility, or status.
- **Easter Egg:** Optional, nonessential, low-frequency product-delight interaction that never gates information or functionality.

## 9. User and Operational Workflows

### `NAV-WF-001` Application identification

1. The platform determines the surface, size, theme, and platform mask.
2. The platform selects the approved mark assigned to that surface.
3. The asset is rendered from the registered source or approved derivative.
4. The platform applies accessible labeling where the mark is interactive.
5. The product does not infer authority, role, or subscription from the selected mark.

### `NAV-WF-002` Compact shell transition

1. A desktop or responsive shell moves from expanded to collapsed state.
2. The full logo is replaced by the approved circuit-horse mark or approved compact mark.
3. The accessible name remains “EquineSync.”
4. Keyboard focus and navigation order remain unchanged.
5. The transition respects reduced-motion settings.

### `NAV-WF-003` Alternate app icon selection

1. An eligible user opens appearance settings.
2. The platform shows only approved alternate icons.
3. The user selects the 9D Circle Monogram or another separately approved icon.
4. The platform confirms the cosmetic change.
5. No role, plan, tenant, environment, or data state changes.
6. The user can restore the default circuit-horse icon.

### `NAV-WF-004` Stead easter-egg interaction

1. A user performs an eligible low-risk interaction.
2. The platform checks feature flag, workflow classification, frequency limit, age/safeguarding rules, and motion preference.
3. The Stead interaction appears only when all checks pass.
4. The interaction never blocks or alters the workflow.
5. The event records a privacy-minimized product event if analytics are authorized.
6. A serious workflow suppresses the interaction.

### `NAV-WF-005` Search empty state

1. A permission-filtered search returns no visible result.
2. The shell displays neutral, practical guidance.
3. A Stead illustration may appear only in an ordinary, non-sensitive context.
4. The message does not imply that a protected or unauthorized record exists.
5. The user receives permitted next steps, such as refining the query or changing the active context.

## 10. Business Rules

### Brand and identity rules

- `NAV-BR-001`: The circuit-horse mark is the primary production app icon.
- `NAV-BR-002`: The 9D Circle Monogram is the secondary compact mark and approved optional alternate app icon.
- `NAV-BR-003`: The 9C Speed Lines Mark is the ultra-small functional favicon and constrained system mark.
- `NAV-BR-004`: The full EquineSync logo is used where the name must be read.
- `NAV-BR-005`: The EquineSync Stead is not a substitute for the formal logo or product mark.
- `NAV-BR-006`: A mark shall not be stretched, skewed, mirrored, rotated, redrawn, or combined with unrelated horse imagery.
- `NAV-BR-007`: Environment, plan, role, authority, billing state, or safety state shall not be communicated by app icon alone.
- `NAV-BR-008`: Generic horse clip art, stock silhouettes, or AI-generated substitute logos are prohibited.

### Typography rules

- `NAV-BR-009`: Bright Demo is reserved for expressive brand headlines, hero messaging, campaign statements, presentation covers, and carefully selected spacious onboarding moments.
- `NAV-BR-010`: Bright Demo shall not be used for navigation, controls, forms, tables, alerts, search results, or dense mobile layouts.
- `NAV-BR-011`: Bodoni Moda is reserved for “In Sync.”, premium emphasis, signature statements, and short luxury moments.
- `NAV-BR-012`: Bodoni Moda shall not be used for long passages, compact labels, forms, or data-heavy interfaces.
- `NAV-BR-013`: Lora is used for body, editorial, documentation, educational, help, and sufficiently spacious product copy.
- `NAV-BR-014`: Lora shall not be forced into compact controls, dense tables, or small navigation where verified legibility is reduced.
- `NAV-BR-015`: No operational utility typeface is authorized until the Founder records a decision.

### Color rules

- `NAV-BR-016`: The shell uses approved brand tokens rather than arbitrary local color values.
- `NAV-BR-017`: Midnight Graphite, Slate Navy, Frost White, and Glacier Silver carry the majority of shell surfaces.
- `NAV-BR-018`: Smoky Lilac and Ice Blue are restrained accents.
- `NAV-BR-019`: Bright yellow from the illustrated Stead reference is not an ordinary shell color.
- `NAV-BR-020`: Yellow may appear only in a separately approved Stead, promotional, campaign, seasonal, or alternate-icon context.
- `NAV-BR-021`: Color is never the sole indicator of status, urgency, permission, error, completion, or selection.

### Stead and easter-egg rules

- `NAV-BR-022`: Stead behavior is optional and nonessential.
- `NAV-BR-023`: Stead behavior shall be absent from serious workflows.
- `NAV-BR-024`: Stead behavior shall respect Full, Reduced, and Off motion settings.
- `NAV-BR-025`: An easter egg shall not unlock records, permissions, paid rights, rewards affecting operations, or hidden functionality.
- `NAV-BR-026`: Search easter eggs shall not imply that an unauthorized result exists.
- `NAV-BR-027`: Easter eggs shall be feature-flagged and disabled by default until separately activated.
- `NAV-BR-028`: A hidden interaction shall occur no more than once per eligible session unless a later approved specification sets a stricter limit.
- `NAV-BR-029`: Idle Stead behavior shall occur no more than once in a 30-minute period.
- `NAV-BR-030`: Repeated completion animation shall be capped and shall not interrupt rapid task work.

## 15. User Interface and Experience Requirements

### Brand character

- `NAV-UI-001`: The shell shall communicate premium quality, quiet luxury, professional equestrian heritage, modern SaaS technology, calm confidence, warmth, and operational precision.
- `NAV-UI-002`: The shell shall not resemble a rustic ranch template, children’s horse game, novelty mascot application, or generic corporate dashboard.
- `NAV-UI-003`: Decorative styling shall remain subordinate to navigation, search, care, scheduling, status, and safety information.

### Approved color tokens

- `NAV-UI-004`: Midnight Graphite `#232734`.
- `NAV-UI-005`: Slate Navy `#2E3550`.
- `NAV-UI-006`: Smoky Lilac `#B8AECF`.
- `NAV-UI-007`: Ice Blue `#DCEAF4`.
- `NAV-UI-008`: Glacier Silver `#E3E6EB`.
- `NAV-UI-009`: Frost White `#F7F8FA`, subject to exact production-token registration.

### Typography

- `NAV-UI-010`: Brand headlines use Bright Demo only where the text remains comfortably readable and the workflow is not dense or urgent.
- `NAV-UI-011`: “In Sync.” uses Bodoni Moda in approved brand compositions.
- `NAV-UI-012`: Lora supports body, documentation, editorial, help, and spacious interface copy.
- `NAV-UI-013`: The shell shall use a Founder-approved operational utility typeface for controls, navigation, search, tables, calendars, badges, timestamps, and dense layouts.
- `NAV-UI-014`: Until `NAV-OQ-001` is resolved, no final implementation-ready typography claim may be made.

### Logo and mark selection

- `NAV-UI-015`: Expanded login, onboarding, formal export, and brand-identification surfaces use the approved full logo when the name must be read.
- `NAV-UI-016`: Collapsed navigation and compact headers use the circuit-horse mark or 9D mark according to verified size.
- `NAV-UI-017`: The primary iOS and Android app icon uses the dark circuit-horse treatment.
- `NAV-UI-018`: The 9D Circle Monogram may be offered as an optional alternate app icon.
- `NAV-UI-019`: The 9C Speed Lines Mark is preferred at 16 pixels and other ultra-small constrained surfaces.
- `NAV-UI-020`: The 9D Circle Monogram is preferred at 32 and 48 pixels unless optical testing proves the simplified circuit horse is superior.
- `NAV-UI-021`: The full wordmark shall not be inserted into a favicon.

### Platform behavior

- `NAV-UI-022`: iOS icon composition shall preserve safe space around ears, nose, tail, raised foreleg, motion marks, and baseline.
- `NAV-UI-023`: Android adaptive-icon composition shall preserve essential details under circular and manufacturer-specific masks.
- `NAV-UI-024`: Monochrome, tinted, notification, and themed variants shall use approved derivatives.
- `NAV-UI-025`: Production and staging environments shall have explicit textual and environmental labeling. Icon differences alone are insufficient.

### EquineSync Stead

- `NAV-UI-026`: The Stead may appear in onboarding, ordinary empty states, low-risk completion moments, help surfaces, and approved hidden interactions.
- `NAV-UI-027`: A five-tap or five-click Stead discovery may display “You found the Stead.” only when separately activated.
- `NAV-UI-028`: An idle Stead may briefly peek, blink, and disappear on eligible low-risk dashboards.
- `NAV-UI-029`: A late-night Stead may appear with restrained quiet-hours messaging.
- `NAV-UI-030`: A Stead may appear in tack, inventory, digital whiteboard, recovered-gear, or similar low-risk contexts.
- `NAV-UI-031`: Search no-result copy shall remain practical and shall not imply the existence of hidden records.
- `NAV-UI-032`: Stead content shall be suppressible by user preference and by workflow classification.

### Serious-workflow restraint

- `NAV-UI-033`: Bright Demo, playful Bodoni emphasis, mascot animation, humorous copy, and easter eggs shall be absent from serious workflows.
- `NAV-UI-034`: Serious workflows shall use direct language, restrained formal marks, verified status patterns, and accessible controls.
- `NAV-UI-035`: A static formal brand mark may appear only when it does not distract from urgent content.

### Accessibility and field use

- `NAV-UI-036`: Every interactive logo or icon shall have an accessible name.
- `NAV-UI-037`: Decorative assets shall be hidden from assistive technology.
- `NAV-UI-038`: Visual status shall include text or semantic equivalents.
- `NAV-UI-039`: Focus, zoom, dynamic text, high contrast, screen readers, keyboard use, touch operation, and reduced motion shall remain supported.
- `NAV-UI-040`: The shell shall remain readable in outdoor glare and usable one-handed.
- `NAV-UI-041`: Motion shall not flash, loop indefinitely, or interfere with input.
- `NAV-UI-042`: Full, Reduced, and Off motion modes shall preserve identical essential information.

## 20. Offline and Synchronization Behavior

- Registered shell assets required for ordinary operation shall be packaged or safely cached.
- Offline mode shall not fetch an unregistered substitute logo or font.
- Font failure shall fall back to an approved stack without hiding content or changing authority.
- Motion and Stead frequency limits shall not reset in a way that causes repeated interruption during unstable connectivity.
- Offline search empty states shall distinguish unavailable synchronization from a true permission-filtered no-result state.
- A stale asset manifest shall not silently activate a superseded mark after revocation.
