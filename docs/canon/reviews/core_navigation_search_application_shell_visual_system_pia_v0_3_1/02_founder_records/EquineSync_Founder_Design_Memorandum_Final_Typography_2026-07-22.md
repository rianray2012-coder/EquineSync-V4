# EquineSync Founder Design Memorandum

**Subject:** Final Typography Selection for the EquineSync Brand System  
**Status:** Founder Approved Design Direction (Typography)  
**Date:** July 22, 2026

## Purpose

This memorandum records the final typography direction selected for the EquineSync brand identity. These typefaces establish the visual voice of the platform and will be used consistently across branding, marketing, product design, documentation, and future design systems.

The objective is to communicate premium quality, quiet luxury, professional equestrian heritage, modern SaaS technology, warmth and approachability, and exceptional readability.

## Final Typography System

### Primary Display Typeface: Bright Demo

**Primary use:** brand headlines, hero statements, marketing campaigns, website landing pages, editorial moments, presentation covers, and premium promotional materials.

**Example:**

> Every Horse.  
> Every Task.

Bright Demo introduces personality and warmth while remaining elegant and memorable. Its handwritten character creates an emotional connection without sacrificing sophistication.

**Avoid:** body paragraphs, navigation, forms, tables, dense UI, alerts, and safety-critical content.

### Brand Signature Typeface: Bodoni Moda

**Primary use:** `In Sync.`, premium emphasis, signature statements, short callouts, luxury brand moments, and select approved logo treatments.

**Example:**

> In Sync.

Bodoni Moda provides timeless elegance and authority. Reserving it for `In Sync.` makes the phrase the visual anchor of the brand.

**Avoid:** long passages, small UI labels, forms, compact data displays, and dense operational interfaces.

### Supporting Typeface: Lora

**Primary use:** body copy, product descriptions, founder letters, blog articles, documentation, long-form educational content, help content, and marketing copy.

Lora offers excellent readability while maintaining a refined editorial character that complements Bright Demo and Bodoni Moda.

**Avoid:** very small controls, compact navigation, dense scheduling grids, and tightly packed data tables.

### Operational Utility Layer

For navigation, controls, search, forms, calendars, tables, badges, timestamps, and dense mobile interfaces, use platform-native system UI sans-serif fonts. Inter is preferred for web when locally hosted, rights-verified, and performance-tested. This utility layer supports function and is not a fourth brand typeface.

## Typography Hierarchy

| Level | Typeface | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Brand headlines | Bright Demo | Emotional, memorable statements |
| Brand signature | Bodoni Moda | `In Sync.`, premium emphasis, luxury moments |
| Body and long-form | Lora | Reading, documentation, editorial and spacious product copy |
| Dense operational UI | Platform-native system UI; locally hosted Inter preferred for web | Navigation, controls, search, tables, calendars, badges and timestamps |

## Founder Decision

The Founder approves Bright Demo as the display typeface, Bodoni Moda as the signature typeface, Lora as the body and long-form typeface, and the defined operational utility layer for compact product interfaces.

This memorandum establishes canonical documentary design direction. It does not by itself authorize font procurement, embedding, redistribution, implementation, deployment, or production activation. Font rights and production binaries must be separately verified and registered.
