# Safeguarding Terminology and Definition Reconciliation

| Term | Specialized meaning | Owning canon | Reconciliation rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Minor | Person below applicable majority subject to verified exceptions | Identity supplies person; safeguarding supplies heightened treatment | Do not infer capacity solely from age |
| Guardian | Verified scoped authority concerning a protected person | Relationship/Claims | Parent, payer, emergency contact, or account holder is not enough |
| Authorized adult | Adult with explicit purpose-limited authority | Relationship/Permission | Not a guardian synonym |
| Caregiver | Actor assigned defined care/supervision activities | Relationship | No implied legal or financial authority |
| Delegated assistant | Distinct attributed actor acting under scoped delegation | Relationship/Permission | No shared account; cannot exceed delegator |
| Safeguarding authority | Authority to receive/review/escalate/restrict safeguarding matters | Claims/Permission plus specialized policy | Separate from platform admin or employment |
| Protected participant | Person receiving heightened safety/privacy treatment | Safeguarding | Does not imply incapacity |
| Report | Submitted allegation or observation | Claims/Audit | Not a fact or finding merely because submitted |
| Indicator | Cue for review | Safeguarding/AI | Not proof, score of guilt, or legal conclusion |
| Restriction | Temporary scoped control | Claims/Permission | Must be reasoned, expiring, and reviewable |
| Prohibited contact | Negative relationship limiting contact or visibility | Relationship | Must propagate through messaging/search/calendar |
| Assent | Participant's affirmative agreement appropriate to capacity | Agreement/Consent | Distinct from guardian consent and authorization |
| Notice | Governed consequential communication | Communication | Delivery and receipt remain separate |
| Sealed record | Record subject to exceptional restricted access | Stewardship/Permission | Not deletion and not inaccessible to all lawful review |
| SafeSport status | External evidence or framework state | External Architecture/Claims | Not EquineSync certification or endorsement |
