# Safeguarding Canon Substantive Review Memorandum

**Analysis class:** `NONAUTHORITATIVE_PRELIMINARY_REVIEW_ANALYSIS`  
**Candidate state:** Non-controlling; not adopted; not locked

## Executive Finding

The original draft established correct constitutional boundaries but was not yet precise enough for later control design. It lacked defined actor terms, multiple-guardian conflict behavior, age-transition rules, youth-account principles, one-to-one communication governance, neutral report-state semantics, external-framework claim limits, record segregation, and a complete offline safeguarding boundary.

The revised draft adds those noncontroversial protections without resolving twelve material founder choices. No P0 defect was found. Twelve P1 adoption blockers remain as explicit founder decisions; six P2 drafting and registry follow-ups remain.

## Existing Founder Direction Incorporated

- identity, account, actor, role, relationship, authority, permission, payer, and guardian remain separate;
- payer and emergency-contact status do not create guardianship;
- guardian evidence informs but does not independently grant field access;
- delegation cannot exceed the delegator's current authority;
- disputes preserve neutral evidence and do not make EquineSync a legal adjudicator;
- emergency restrictions are narrow, temporary, attributable, and reviewable;
- minors and safeguarding information require heightened privacy and minimum-necessary projection;
- AI cannot make consequential safeguarding, credibility, guardianship, or disciplinary decisions;
- offline data remains actor-, barn-, device-, and session-scoped;
- external providers and frameworks do not create EquineSync authority.

## Constitutional Ownership

This candidate should own heightened safety constraints, protected-participant treatment, safeguarding case semantics, anti-retaliation, minor communication boundaries, and non-delegable safeguarding decisions. It must not own durable identity, relationship creation, final permission evaluation, legal adjudication, record retention, delivery proof, external credentials, or platform incident operations.

## Recommendation

Retain the revised draft as the controlled review candidate. Obtain founder decisions SG-FD01 through SG-FD12, then apply only approved language and repeat cross-canon review. Do not advance to adoption or lock before those decisions and exact companion-source recovery.
