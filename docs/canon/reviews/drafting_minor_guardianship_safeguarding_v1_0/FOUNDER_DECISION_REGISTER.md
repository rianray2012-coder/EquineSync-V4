| SG-FD01 | How must EquineSync handle a participant reaching majority? | Changes guardian access, consent, account control, notices, local data, and historical records | Require verified transition, notice, permission recalculation, consent review, and adult control without erasing history | Automatic cutoff risks safety and notice failure; automatic continuation violates adult autonomy | Identity, Relationship, Permission, Consent, Stewardship; clarifies existing continuity doctrine | Later workflow and tests; no runtime authority | "Reaching majority shall trigger a verified transition and permission recalculation; prior guardian access shall not continue or terminate solely by unattended age arithmetic." |
| SG-FD02 | What is the default when verified guardians issue conflicting instructions? | Safety, access, transport, media, and communication may conflict | Freeze only the disputed consequential action, preserve emergency safety, and route to conflict-screened human review | Latest-write is unsafe; universal freeze may block care | Claims, Relationship, Permission; new scoped rule | Later review queue and restriction states | "Conflicting guardian instructions shall not be resolved by recency, payment, account rank, or role label." |
| SG-FD03 | When may an adult send a one-to-one message to a minor? | High grooming and boundary risk; affects messages and attachments | Permit only approved relationship/purpose with guardian visibility or organization oversight, reporting controls, and auditable metadata | Total ban harms legitimate coaching; unrestricted contact creates safety risk | Communication, Relationship, Permission; genuinely new | Later messaging policy and projection tests | "One-to-one adult/minor communication requires an approved safeguarding policy, active relationship, permitted purpose, observable accountability, and immediate reporting controls." |
| SG-FD04 | What are the defaults for minor media, live location, public search, and recommendations? | Exposes identity, whereabouts, schedule, and imagery | Default deny public discovery, live-location sharing, and promotional media unless specific verified consent and safeguarding policy allow | Opt-out increases exposure; universal prohibition may block legitimate event use | Media, Search, Consent, Permission; new specialized rule | Later privacy defaults and index exclusions | "Minor and protected-participant discovery, location, and media shall be private by default and purpose-limited when expressly authorized." |
| SG-FD05 | What emergency intervention may EquineSync support before guardian contact? | Affects immediate safety, disclosure, notices, and temporary restrictions | Allow minimum necessary safety routing and temporary controls, with expiry, failed-contact evidence, and mandatory review | No action risks harm; broad override creates durable authority drift | Claims, Communication, Incident, Permission; clarifies existing emergency doctrine | Later break-glass policy; no external activation | "Imminent-risk controls shall be narrow, temporary, attributable, minimum necessary, and incapable of creating durable authority." |
| SG-FD06 | How should mandated-reporting obligations be governed? | Jurisdiction-specific legal duty; affects reporters, reviewers, subjects, and evidence | EquineSync routes to qualified designated humans using jurisdiction policy and never claims to decide the legal duty automatically | Automated filing risks legal error; no support risks missed escalation | Claims, Incident, Communication, Audit; genuinely new | Later jurisdiction registry and human escalation | "EquineSync shall not autonomously determine or represent that a mandated-reporting duty exists." |
| SG-FD07 | Who may access, seal, unseal, amend, or produce safeguarding records? | Allegations and reporter identity are highly sensitive | Conflict-screened role separation, segmented evidence, audited access, non-destructive correction, and founder-approved unsealing classes | Broad admin access leaks; absolute sealing blocks due process and safety | Stewardship, Audit, Claims, Permission; new specialized rule | Later case permission matrix | "Safeguarding records shall be segregated and accessible only through purpose-specific, conflict-screened, audited authority." |
| SG-FD08 | What may EquineSync say about background checks and credential status? | Could imply safety certification or professional fitness | Display source, scope, date, expiration, limitations, and verification state; prohibit universal `safe` or `cleared` claims | Simple badge is legible but overclaims; no display loses useful evidence | External Architecture, Claims, Security; clarifies provider-neutrality doctrine | Later status vocabulary | "A background-check or credential status shall not be represented as a guarantee of safety, suitability, or continuous compliance." |
| SG-FD09 | Which capabilities are allowed for minor and guardian-managed accounts? | Affects autonomy, privacy, messaging, purchases, location, agreements, and exports | Adopt age/capability tiers with minimum necessary access and human-reviewable exceptions | One minor role is too coarse; full adult parity is unsafe | Identity, Permission, Financial, Agreement; new | Later capability registry | "Minor-facing capability shall be explicitly tiered and shall not inherit adult authority through account creation or guardian management." |
| SG-FD10 | How are mistaken, unsubstantiated, withdrawn, and malicious reports treated? | Balances good-faith reporting, due process, and anti-retaliation | Keep distinct states; require evidence-based human finding for maliciousness; never equate unsubstantiated with false | Aggressive penalties chill reports; no abuse process permits harassment | Claims, Audit, Stewardship; new | Later case states and appeal workflow | "An unsubstantiated, mistaken, duplicate, or withdrawn report shall not be labeled malicious without a separate evidence-based human determination." |
| SG-FD11 | What communication observability standard applies to adult/minor exchanges? | Must balance safety evidence and privacy | Preserve metadata and reportability by default; content access only under defined safeguarding authority and audit | Full surveillance is intrusive; metadata-only may miss content risk | Communication, Privacy, Audit, Security; new | Later retention/access controls | "Safeguarding observability shall be disclosed, purpose-limited, role-separated, and no broader than necessary for safety and accountability." |
| SG-FD12 | How may SafeSport or similar frameworks be referenced? | Certification and legal-compliance overclaim risk | Permit scoped evidence and interoperability claims only; prohibit endorsement or certification claims without authoritative proof | Avoiding all references loses interoperability; broad claims create liability | External Architecture, Claims, Communication; clarifies existing vendor boundary | Later adapter vocabulary only | "External safeguarding frameworks may supply evidence or routing requirements but do not confer EquineSync authority, certification, or compliance status." |
