## 1. Ownership Rule

Each constitutional concept has one primary owner. Peer and subordinate documents may impose narrower controls, supply domain facts, or reference the owner, but must not create a competing lifecycle, definition, authority, or system of record.

## 3. Conflict Handling

When two documents appear to own the same concept:

1. preserve both texts and provenance;
2. identify the primary owner in this registry;
3. convert the non-owner's rule into a reference or narrower enforcement rule;
4. prohibit silent deletion of historical language;
5. record the reconciliation in the Cross-Canon Audit and Conflict Register;
6. require founder or delegated constitutional approval for any ownership change.

## 4. Authority Boundary

This register resolves constitutional ownership for drafting and audit. It does not authorize schema changes, migrations, implementation, provider activation, production access, or public launch.
