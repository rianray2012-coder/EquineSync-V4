# Master Relationship Model v2.0 Dependency Proposals

These are proposed dependency updates only. They do not open or implement an RF
and do not amend the current RF or ATLAS records before founder lock.

## RF31

```markdown
Canonical dependency: `MASTER_RELATIONSHIP_MODEL.md` Version 2.0.

RF31 must implement Horse Transfer and Passport Continuity using explicit,
versioned, purpose-limited relationship transitions. It must honor delegated
authority, scoped authority conflicts, relationship origin and promotion,
dependencies, suspension effects, succession, inactive-account continuity,
reverification, restrictive authority edges, sensitive relationship metadata,
consent effects, relationship-aware notice eligibility, and downstream impact
events. Claims procedure, record handling, and final authorization remain owned
by their respective canons. RF27 retains physical intake and location ownership.
```

## RF32

```markdown
Canonical dependency: `MASTER_RELATIONSHIP_MODEL.md` Version 2.0.

RF32 must model payment and financial responsibility without collapsing payer,
owner, guardian, guarantor, invoice recipient, payment-method owner, settlement
source, refund recipient, beneficiary, dispute claimant, provider, or recipient
roles. It must honor delegated financial authority, purpose limitation,
relationship dependencies, suspension effects, succession, inactive-account
continuity, reverification, restricted contacts, consent effects, notification
eligibility, and relationship impact events. Payment status cannot change
ownership, guardianship, emergency duties, record stewardship, or Passport
continuity.
```

## ATLAS5

```markdown
ATLAS5 external-service readiness is downstream of the founder-locked Master
Relationship Model Version 2.0. External vendors may consume purpose-limited,
permission-filtered, policy-versioned relationship projections and impact-event
intents, but may not create, broaden, resolve, promote, suspend, restrict,
succeed, or supersede EquineSync relationship authority. Provider delivery or
execution does not prove consent, acceptance, authority, stewardship, or legal
effect. RF33-RF36 remain proposed and unopened.
```
