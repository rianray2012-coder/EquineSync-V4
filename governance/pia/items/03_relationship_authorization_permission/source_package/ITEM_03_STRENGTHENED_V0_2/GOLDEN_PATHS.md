# Golden Paths

**Status:** `DOCUMENTARY_REPRODUCTION_PATHS_NOT_EXECUTED`

## RAP-GP-001 - Bounded owner delegation for a care task

An authenticated owner acts as self, selects a current horse and care-task purpose, proposes a time-bound delegation to an identified accountable actor, and reviews action/resource/field/time restrictions. Required acceptance occurs. Item 03 verifies source ownership/custody authority, relationship, representation, delegation scope, restrictions, and watermark, then returns a minimum projection to Item 07. Revocation invalidates later action. Evidence reconstructs each version and actor.

## RAP-GP-002 - Guardian-authorized participant action

An authenticated guardian presents a current, purpose-scoped representation basis for a protected participant. Item 03 validates guardian/minor boundaries, agreement/consent, restrictions, safeguarding state, tenant/context, and current versions. Item 08 receives only the permitted participation projection. Shared address, payment, account association, or relationship label alone would deny. Protective restriction takes precedence and is auditable.

## RAP-GP-003 - Facility-context operational access

Item 02 supplies tenant, facility, organization, asset, and location identifiers. Item 03 separately evaluates the actor's relationship, representation/delegation, action, purpose, restrictions, and current policy. The result is bounded to the requested operational action. Facility or organization association, role label, asset possession, or schedule assignment alone cannot grant access.

## RAP-GP-004 - Provider horse-specific access

Canonical provider and accountable-human identities exist. Item 03 validates the provider relationship, representation, accepted delegation when required, horse-specific purpose, time, restrictions, source versions, and watermark. Item 07 coordinates care and Item 10 presents the portal surface under the minimum projection; Item 09 retains payment truth. Profile, API, appointment, care note, invoice, payment, or portal visibility alone cannot create authority.

## RAP-GP-005 - Approved support access

A named support actor references an approved ticket, represented product-support principal, narrow purpose, tenant, resource/fields, start/expiry, and accountable approver. Item 03 returns a time-limited minimum projection with notice and review obligations. There is no invisible impersonation. Expiry or revocation invalidates the projection, and evidence preserves the complete actor chain.

## Common completion evidence

Each path requires source identifiers and versions, accountable actors, tenant/context, request, purpose, restrictions, decision, projection, watermark, time, notice, revocation behavior, correction/supersession, and correlation. These are documentary paths for future compliant review; no implementation or runtime execution occurred.
