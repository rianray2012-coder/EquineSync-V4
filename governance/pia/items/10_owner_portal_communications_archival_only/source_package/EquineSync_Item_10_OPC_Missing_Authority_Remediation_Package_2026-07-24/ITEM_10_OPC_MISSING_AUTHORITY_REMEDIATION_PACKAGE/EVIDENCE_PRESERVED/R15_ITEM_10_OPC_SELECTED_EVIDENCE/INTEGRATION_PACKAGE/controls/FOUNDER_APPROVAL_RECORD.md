The Founder approved `OPC-FD-001` through `OPC-FD-023` as recommended.

The Founder approved `OPC-FD-024` with this modification:

> Owner-to-owner messaging is included in the initial controlled scope for community-centered barns, but it must be limited to verified owners within the same barn or facility.

The approved owner-to-owner capability is not a public social network. It remains subject to facility enablement, current relationship verification, tenant and facility isolation, privacy, safeguarding, moderation, blocking, reporting, anti-enumeration, access revocation, audit, and platform safety controls.
