# OPC Peer Attachment Initial-Scope Decision

Decision ID: `OPC-FD-025-PROPOSED`

Status: `PROPOSED_FOR_FOUNDER_EXECUTION`

## Proposed decision

Same-facility owner-to-owner text messaging may remain within the initial controlled community capability family. Peer-to-peer attachments are excluded from the initial community activation slice and remain independently disabled.

Peer attachment support may be introduced only through a later controlled change that defines and approves:

- permitted formats and size limits;
- malware scanning and quarantine;
- metadata treatment;
- sensitivity, consent, destination, and independent access permission;
- signed access, expiry, revocation, and download limitations;
- privacy, safeguarding, age, and guardian controls;
- reporting, retention, evidence, monitoring, rollback, and kill-switch behavior;
- executable tests and activation evidence;
- a separate Founder disposition.

## Proposed finding disposition

`OPC_FIND_P2_001_CLOSED_BY_APPROVED_INITIAL_SCOPE_PEER_ATTACHMENTS_DEFERRED`

This decision does not restrict separately governed owner-to-facility service-request attachments or formal document workflows that satisfy their own contracts and activation gates.
