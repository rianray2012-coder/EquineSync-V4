## 3. APPROVED DESIGN DIRECTION

Incorporate the approved recommendations for `FAC-FD-001` through `FAC-FD-018`, including at minimum the following controlling directions:

1. Facility, Tenant, Organization, Barn, and Business remain distinct concepts with explicitly defined meanings and responsibilities.

2. Tenant remains the application data-isolation boundary.

3. Organizations may control or associate with multiple Tenants only through explicit, auditable, evidence-supported relationships.

4. A physical Facility may be associated with multiple Tenants only through explicit, time-bounded relationships that do not weaken Tenant isolation.

5. Facility areas use a flexible hierarchical model rather than a rigid universal hierarchy.

6. Multiple Organization types may be represented as first-class types under a controlled registry.

7. Lifecycle states must be explicit. Suspension, closure, restoration, and reactivation must not occur silently.

8. Transfers, mergers, splits, closures, and successor arrangements must preserve lineage and must not automatically cascade ownership, authority, stewardship, access, or responsibility.

9. User context selection must be explicit where multiple valid contexts exist and must be auditable.

10. Other domains may consume only bounded, versioned reference facts rather than directly mutating Facility-domain topology.

11. Memberships, employment relationships, staffing assignments, delegations, and similar relationship facts remain owned by the appropriate Relationship or authorization domain rather than being absorbed into the Facility domain.

12. Stewardship and authority must be established through valid evidence, relationships, delegations, ownership, leases, agreements, or other recognized authority sources. Payment status alone does not create stewardship or authority.

13. Provider and vendor representation must preserve the distinction between the provider organization, the service relationship, the responsible human actor, and the customer or facility context.

14. Addresses, locale, time zone, and physical-location facts must be modeled explicitly and must not be treated as interchangeable.

15. Duplicate detection, reconciliation, and merger must be controlled, evidence-backed, reversible where required, and must preserve lineage.

16. Public Facility profiles must remain a separate, revocable projection and must not expose private Tenant topology or create authority.

17. First-time creation and onboarding must be adaptive, context-sensitive, and must not force individual horse owners to invent Facility or Organization structures.

18. Legacy records that cannot be safely reconciled must be quarantined or explicitly classified rather than silently normalized into the new model.

Use the exact recommendation language from the verified Founder Decision Register wherever available. Do not replace detailed approved wording with this summary when the controlling package contains more precise language.
