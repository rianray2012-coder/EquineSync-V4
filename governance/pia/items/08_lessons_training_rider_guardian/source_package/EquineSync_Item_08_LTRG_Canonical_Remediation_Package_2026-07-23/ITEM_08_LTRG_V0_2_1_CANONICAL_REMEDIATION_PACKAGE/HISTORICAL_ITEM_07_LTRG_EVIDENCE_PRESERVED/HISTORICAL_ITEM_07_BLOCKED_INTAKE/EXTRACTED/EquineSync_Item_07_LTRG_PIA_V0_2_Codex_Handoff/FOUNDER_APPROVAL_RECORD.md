# EquineSync Founder Approval Record

## Artifact family

Lessons, Training, Rider, and Guardian Privacy and Implementation Assessment (LTRG PIA)

## Founder disposition

`LTRG-FD-001` through `LTRG-FD-020` are approved as recommended.

## Approval date

July 22, 2026

## Authorized effect

This approval establishes the pre-draft product and governance direction for the combined Lessons, Training, Rider, and Guardian PIA, including:

- separate authoritative lesson and horse-training records;
- support for facility-associated, independent, and multi-facility trainer models;
- distinct rider profiles and relationship-based guardian authority;
- safeguarding-controlled communications involving minors;
- human-controlled rider, horse, and pairing suitability decisions;
- explicit record-level visibility classes;
- controlled lesson and training states, substitutions, and evidence;
- safety, consent, offline, media, AI, and cross-PIA boundaries.

## Authority limitation

The approval authorizes documentary drafting, review, and repository integration only. It does **not** authorize implementation, application-code changes, schemas, migrations, deployment, production use, pilot enrollment, first-user enrollment, or autonomous safety, safeguarding, medical, legal, suitability, or financial decisions.
