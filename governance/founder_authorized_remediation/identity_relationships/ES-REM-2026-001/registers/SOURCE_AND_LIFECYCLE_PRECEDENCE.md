# Source and Lifecycle Precedence

## Controlling interpretation

1. Historical source, decision, recommendation, PIA, and pre-ratification records remain point-in-time evidence. Their bytes and historical status are not rewritten.
2. For current canon-source identity and lifecycle only, later checksum-backed adoption and lock records at the repository baseline control over earlier `PENDING`, unresolved-path, or pre-lock statements.
3. Founder approval of an architecture recommendation or as-designed PIA does not ratify the exact formal ADR wording.
4. The completed review disposition controls the need for this bounded remediation but does not approve any of the 19 proposed redlines.
5. The Founder authorization recorded in `FOUNDER_AUTHORIZATION.md` authorizes one documentary package only. It does not ratify, adopt, freeze, lock, implement, execute, or close F-0001.
6. Candidate overlays control over conflicting predecessor wording within the candidate files only. They have no effect on the immutable predecessor.
7. A fresh segregated review is required before any new ratification request. Exact owning-domain concurrence and express Founder approval remain required where identified.
