# Automation Accountability Candidate Control

**Status:** `PROPOSED_NOT_APPROVED_PENDING_FRESH_REVIEW`

Whenever a service account, integration, scheduled process, automated workflow, or AI-assisted process participates in a first-user, pilot, internal-build, or general-production flow, it must have a named purpose, owning organization, accountable human owner, bounded credential class, environment scope, versioned policy, monitoring, and revocation path.

This obligation applies at the first point of participation. A later release classification cannot defer accountable-human ownership for automation already used in an earlier flow. The automated actor and accountable human owner are separately attributable; the human owner is not misrepresented as the executing machine actor.

No automation is authorized or activated by this candidate control.
