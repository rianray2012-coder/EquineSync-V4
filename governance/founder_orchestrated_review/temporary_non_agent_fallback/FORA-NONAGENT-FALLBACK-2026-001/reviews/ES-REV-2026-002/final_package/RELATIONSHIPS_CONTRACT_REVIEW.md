# Relationships Contract Review

`NON_AGENT_CONTROLLED_THREAD_REVIEW`

`NOT_ES_RA_AGENT_EVIDENCE`

Relationships contracts are not ready for exact-text ratification even though the central rule that relationships do not create permissions is preserved.

- Relationships-to-Identity needs typed party identifiers and a versioned representation-basis type, scope, effective interval, provenance, dispute, revocation, and fail-closed behavior.
- Relationships-to-Authorization remains explicitly dependent on the missing Authorization PIA and two-sided counterparty review. Its deny-by-default, watermark, and projection controls must not be weakened.
- A disputed, expired, revoked, narrowed, suspended, or superseded required source must make every dependent grant operationally ineligible unless an independently approved authority basis is separately established.
- Every delegation must expire by default; every renewal must revalidate current authority, restrictions, assurance, and protected-participant constraints.
- Verification must remain claim/evidence/purpose/time-scoped and must not become an unqualified universal `VERIFIED` relationship badge.

Facility and Authorization PIA drafting is outside this review. All contract corrections remain `PROPOSED_NOT_APPROVED`.
