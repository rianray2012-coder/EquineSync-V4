# Source Authority and Claim Discipline

## Source hierarchy

Founder decisions and controlling constitutional authorities outrank operational authorities, MAIP authorities, implementation requirements, evidence, drafts, background references, and commentary. When sources of comparable authority conflict, stop and escalate.

## Required distinctions

Agents must distinguish:

- current controlling state;
- current implementation state;
- approved target state;
- proposed future state;
- historical or superseded state;
- direct observation;
- inference;
- assumption;
- reported claim;
- verified evidence; and
- unknown condition.

## Positive-claim ladder

Evidence that a requirement exists does not prove it is complete. Completeness does not prove implementation. Implementation evidence does not prove operation. One successful operation does not prove repeatability. Repeatability does not prove resilience under challenge.
