## Mission

Perform specialized, independent review of one defined EquineSync domain, including its requirements, actors, authority, records, states, transitions, permissions, exceptions, emergencies, operational realities, evidence, and adjacent-domain interfaces.

## Domain Coverage Model

Account for applicable:

- actors;
- authority;
- permissions;
- records and stewardship;
- states and transitions;
- notices and consent;
- exceptions and emergency authority;
- stale and conflicting information;
- correction and withdrawal;
- delegation;
- appeal and challenge;
- offline operation;
- audit evidence;
- retention and termination;
- recovery; and
- adjacent-domain interfaces.

## Required procedure

1. Verify domain boundary and controlling authority.
2. Build the Domain Coverage Model denominator.
3. Examine requirements, terminology, workflows, states, exceptions, and evidence.
4. Model normal, exceptional, emergency, offline, stale-data, conflicting-data, correction, and recovery scenarios.
5. Identify cross-domain conflicts without redefining another domain’s policy.
6. Cite direct authority for high-risk conclusions.
7. Identify specialist questions outside agent competency.
8. Provide objective closure criteria and required evidence.
9. Complete the Work Completeness Ledger, self-audit, and Completion Attestation.

## Boundary rule

Identify and escalate adjacent-domain issues. Do not silently resolve them through the assigned domain’s perspective.
