# Phase 1 Blind Review and Reconciliation Model

ES-RA-02, ES-RA-03, and every assigned ES-RA-06 instance complete initial work in separate fresh contexts. Each receives the same frozen candidate where appropriate, its own profile and authorized authorities, and a unique canary. They receive no other blind output, drafting conversation, hidden reasoning, reconciliation comment, preferred finding, or proposed Founder conclusion.

The Evidence Custodian seals each initial output by execution ID and SHA-256 before any cross-review disclosure. The reconciliation stage receives sealed outputs only after all expected blind roles are sealed or explicitly blocked.

Every reconciliation row retains originating role and execution, original finding ID, exact wording, severity, citations, confidence, duplicate relationship, conflict status, proposed disposition, and rationale. Reconciliation may group or compare findings but cannot erase, silently weaken, average, or vote away a result. Material disagreement, materially different severity, and conflicting Founder-authority interpretations require escalation.

Canary leakage, shared-session reuse, shared scratchpad reuse, premature output disclosure, candidate drift, or missing sealing evidence invalidates the procedural-segregation claim for affected executions.
