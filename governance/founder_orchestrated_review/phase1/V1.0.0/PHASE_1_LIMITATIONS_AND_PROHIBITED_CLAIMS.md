# Phase 1 Limitations and Prohibited Claims

## Limitations

- Role Configuration and Execution Identity do not establish a separate Reviewer Identity.
- Shared models, providers, runtimes, operators, repositories, and organizational control may produce correlated blind spots.
- Procedural isolation depends on both technical controls and preserved evidence; instruction-only boundaries must be labeled as such.
- LLM output is not byte-deterministic. Replay proves reproduced conditions and measured variance only.
- A deterministic pass proves only the executed checks against the recorded bytes and environment.
- Synthetic results do not prove production behavior, customer-data handling, legal compliance, or operational readiness.
- Founder oversight is a decision control, not external independence.

## Prohibited claims

Do not claim or imply: independent human reviewers; external reviewers; separately employed or organizationally independent reviewers; cryptographically distinct persons; legal or regulatory auditors; external assurance; independent audit; certification; ratification; production readiness; activation; implementation authority; release authority; MIAP integration; provider corroboration; or that prior failed runtime evidence became successful retroactively.

Do not use `PROCEDURALLY_SEGREGATED_INTERNAL_AI_REVIEW` unless every required Level 3 control has passed for the exact cycle. Do not convert absence of detected defects into proof of absence.
