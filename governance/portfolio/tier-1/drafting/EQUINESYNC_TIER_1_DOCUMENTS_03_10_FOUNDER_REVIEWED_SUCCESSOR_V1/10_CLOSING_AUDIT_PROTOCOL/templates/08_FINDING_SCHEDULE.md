# Finding Schedule

## Document Control

- Template ID: `T1FD-AUDIT-TEMPLATE-08`
- Template name: `FINDING_SCHEDULE`
- Authority boundary: `NOT_ADOPTED; NOT_ACTIVE; IMPLEMENTATION_NOT_AUTHORIZED; PRODUCTION_USE_NOT_AUTHORIZED; MERGE_NOT_AUTHORIZED; CERTIFICATION_NOT_COMPLETE; FOUNDER_REVIEW_REQUIRED; UNRESOLVED_ITEMS_REMAIN_OPEN_AS_IDENTIFIED`
- First-party status: `FIRST_PARTY_DOCUMENTARY_INSTRUMENT_ONLY`
- Disposition state: `NO_DISPOSITION_SELECTED`

## Purpose

Finding schedule.

## Triggering Event

A finding requires tracking, disposition, or closure evidence.

## Required Inputs

Finding ID; severity; affected path; evidence; owner state; due basis.

## Required Evidence

Finding register row; source review; validation evidence.

## Exclusions

No closure without direct current-package evidence.

## Responsible Preparer

Finding register preparer.

## Required Reviewer

Governance reviewer.

## Approval Or Acknowledgement Field

Finding disposition acknowledgement only; blank until an authenticated Founder or reviewer action is supplied.

## Authority Effect

Records disposition evidence only. This template does not authorize adoption, activation, implementation, production use, certification completion, merge, or protected-branch mutation.

## Prohibited Conclusions

Do not imply Founder acceptance, waiver, or closure authority.

## Completion Criteria

Every finding has status, evidence, residual risk, and closure authority.

## Reopening Or Supersession Effect

Reopened on regression or invalid evidence.

## Instrument-Specific Control

The preparer must record a concrete evidence locator for this instrument's purpose and must mark the row `INCOMPLETE` if the locator is absent.
