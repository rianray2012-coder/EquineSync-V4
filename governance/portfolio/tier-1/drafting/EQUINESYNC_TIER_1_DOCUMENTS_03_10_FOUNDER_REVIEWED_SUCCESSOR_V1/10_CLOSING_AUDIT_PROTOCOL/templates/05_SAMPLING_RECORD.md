# Sampling Record

## Document Control

- Template ID: `T1FD-AUDIT-TEMPLATE-05`
- Template name: `SAMPLING_RECORD`
- Authority boundary: `NOT_ADOPTED; NOT_ACTIVE; IMPLEMENTATION_NOT_AUTHORIZED; PRODUCTION_USE_NOT_AUTHORIZED; MERGE_NOT_AUTHORIZED; CERTIFICATION_NOT_COMPLETE; FOUNDER_REVIEW_REQUIRED; UNRESOLVED_ITEMS_REMAIN_OPEN_AS_IDENTIFIED`
- First-party status: `FIRST_PARTY_DOCUMENTARY_INSTRUMENT_ONLY`
- Disposition state: `NO_DISPOSITION_SELECTED`

## Purpose

Sampling record.

## Triggering Event

A bounded sample is used instead of full population review.

## Required Inputs

Population definition; sample size; sampling method; seed or selection basis.

## Required Evidence

Population register; sampled rows; selection record.

## Exclusions

No claim of full-population validation from a sample.

## Responsible Preparer

Sampling preparer.

## Required Reviewer

Governance reviewer.

## Approval Or Acknowledgement Field

Sample acknowledgement only; blank until an authenticated Founder or reviewer action is supplied.

## Authority Effect

Limits, rather than expands, assurance. This template does not authorize adoption, activation, implementation, production use, certification completion, merge, or protected-branch mutation.

## Prohibited Conclusions

Do not imply sampled review proves unsampled rows.

## Completion Criteria

Sample basis is reproducible and limitations are explicit.

## Reopening Or Supersession Effect

Superseded when the population changes or full review replaces sample.

## Instrument-Specific Control

The preparer must record a concrete evidence locator for this instrument's purpose and must mark the row `INCOMPLETE` if the locator is absent.
