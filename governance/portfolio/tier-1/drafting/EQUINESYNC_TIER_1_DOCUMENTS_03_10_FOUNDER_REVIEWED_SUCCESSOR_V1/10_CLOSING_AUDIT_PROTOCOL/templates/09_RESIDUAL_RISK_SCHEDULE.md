# Residual Risk Schedule

## Document Control

- Template ID: `T1FD-AUDIT-TEMPLATE-09`
- Template name: `RESIDUAL_RISK_SCHEDULE`
- Authority boundary: `NOT_ADOPTED; NOT_ACTIVE; IMPLEMENTATION_NOT_AUTHORIZED; PRODUCTION_USE_NOT_AUTHORIZED; MERGE_NOT_AUTHORIZED; CERTIFICATION_NOT_COMPLETE; FOUNDER_REVIEW_REQUIRED; UNRESOLVED_ITEMS_REMAIN_OPEN_AS_IDENTIFIED`
- First-party status: `FIRST_PARTY_DOCUMENTARY_INSTRUMENT_ONLY`
- Disposition state: `NO_DISPOSITION_SELECTED`

## Purpose

Residual-risk schedule.

## Triggering Event

A remediated or deferred item leaves residual risk.

## Required Inputs

Risk ID; affected item; likelihood basis; impact basis; proposed treatment.

## Required Evidence

Risk source; mitigation record; decision dependency.

## Exclusions

No accepted-risk label without named accepting authority.

## Responsible Preparer

Risk preparer.

## Required Reviewer

Founder or assigned risk reviewer.

## Approval Or Acknowledgement Field

Risk review acknowledgement only; blank until an authenticated Founder or reviewer action is supplied.

## Authority Effect

Does not accept risk by itself. This template does not authorize adoption, activation, implementation, production use, certification completion, merge, or protected-branch mutation.

## Prohibited Conclusions

Do not imply bulk acceptance or indefinite waiver.

## Completion Criteria

Each risk has individual treatment and Founder-action flag.

## Reopening Or Supersession Effect

Reopened at expiry, material change, or rejection.

## Instrument-Specific Control

The preparer must record a concrete evidence locator for this instrument's purpose and must mark the row `INCOMPLETE` if the locator is absent.
