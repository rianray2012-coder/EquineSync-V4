## Executive assessment

**The governance posture is right, and that is the most important thing here.** V2 does not claim to have fixed what it has not fixed. `second_reviewer_state=NOT_PERFORMED_NOT_FABRICATED` on all 20 consolidated findings; `authority_effect=NONE` on all 20; `merge_effect=MERGE_NOT_AUTHORIZED` on all 20; no Founder disposition recorded anywhere. The determination string has gone from `…_COMPLETE_READY_FOR_…_FINAL_…` to `REVISION_REQUIRED_PACKAGING_READY_FOR_BOUNDED_REREVIEW_ACCEPTED_CONTENT_REVISION_REQUIRED`. All three source reviews are preserved verbatim under `OUTSIDE_REVIEW/SOURCE_REVIEWS/` rather than summarised away. The self-closure pattern that was the substance of R2-05 is gone, and the README says so in terms: the package "does not adopt, activate, implement, deploy, merge, certify, waive, accept risk, or close high-consequence findings by self-review." That is a genuine correction of behaviour, not a wording change.

**Understand what V2 is, though.** It is an additive consolidation and disposition layer. It records the findings; it does not repair the operative registers. Every underlying defect from the previous review is still present byte-for-byte:

- `FD-T1R2-001` still reads "eleven-state" in `FOUNDER_DECISION_DISPOSITION_REGISTER.csv` line 2, the matching `.json` line 21, and `FOUNDER_DECISION_PACKET.csv` line 2. The matrix still holds thirteen.
- `FD-T1R2-004` still reads "For each open item in `06_FINDINGS_RISKS_EXCEPTIONS_WAIVERS/`…" verbatim.
- Document 06 still holds 9 rows, all `record_classification=finding`, zero risk/exception/waiver, one distinct `closure_evidence` value across all nine, `second_reviewer_not_assigned=YES` on all nine.

That is defensible under V2's stated posture — but `PARTIALLY_REMEDIATED`, applied to 10 of 20 findings, is carrying more weight than the evidence supports. For T1C-011 the supporting evidence is "V1 contains 13-state matrix," which describes the pre-existing state, not a remediation. Nobody reading the register quickly should come away thinking half the findings are half-fixed.

**Two blocking defects were introduced by this build.** The package fails its own integrity check, and it ships a passing validation report that its own validator contradicts. Both are build-process failures rather than governance failures, and both are fixable in one pass — but a governance package that cannot verify its own bytes cannot be the basis of an independent closure re-review.

## What improved, specifically

Worth recording, because it is substantial and it is the right direction:

- **Self-closure eliminated.** `NOT_PERFORMED_NOT_FABRICATED` on all 20 findings, and the Second Reviewer's review is explicitly not invented (T1C-019). This was the governance failure behind R2-05, and it is corrected.
- **Determination string is now honest.** `REVISION_REQUIRED_PACKAGING_READY_FOR_BOUNDED_REREVIEW_ACCEPTED_CONTENT_REVISION_REQUIRED` contains no `COMPLETE`, no `FINAL`, no `READY`. R2-04 and the earlier kit finding F-04 are addressed.
- **Source reviews preserved verbatim.** All three under `OUTSIDE_REVIEW/SOURCE_REVIEWS/`, not summarised. A closure re-reviewer can check the consolidation against the originals.
- **Validator surface expanded 212 → 389 checks**, and it correctly caught V2-01. The validator is doing real work — it is the shipped *report* that is wrong, not the tool.
- **`STRUCTURAL_PASS` vs `SUBSTANTIVE_CONTROL_BLOCKED`** as distinct statuses, plus negative fixtures under `VALIDATION/FIXTURES`. This is the direct answer to "validator PASS is not substantive assurance," and it is the correct answer.
- **No Founder disposition preselected** anywhere, and `authority_effect=NONE` throughout.
- **Custody record** with PR #83 tip `1c053c4a…` and an as-of timestamp, addressing part of R2-12 and R2-16.

Fix V2-01 and V2-02 before anything else — they are a single rebuild, and until they are fixed no independent closure re-review can proceed on an artifact that fails its own integrity check. Then V2-03 and V2-04, which are the difference between a re-reviewer being able to work from the CSVs and having to re-derive everything from the markdown.

**Independent closure re-review should not be commissioned until V2-01 and V2-02 are cleared.** Sending a package that fails `sha256sum -c` to an outside reviewer will consume the engagement on the integrity break and produce no substantive assurance — which is how the last two review cycles were spent.
