## Purpose

Reviewer independence disclosure.

## Triggering Event

A reviewer performs or contributes to documentary review.

## Required Inputs

Reviewer identity or role; relationship; tooling used; independence limits.

## Required Evidence

Disclosure statement; review assignment; conflict cross-reference.

## Exclusions

No third-party or independent certification unless separately evidenced.

## Responsible Preparer

Reviewer.

## Required Reviewer

Founder or assigned governance reviewer.

## Approval Or Acknowledgement Field

Disclosure acknowledgement only; blank until an authenticated Founder or reviewer action is supplied.

## Authority Effect

Discloses limits only. This template does not authorize adoption, activation, implementation, production use, certification completion, merge, or protected-branch mutation.

## Prohibited Conclusions

Do not claim independence when review is Founder-directed or machine-assisted.

## Completion Criteria

All limitations are recorded and unresolved limitations remain open.

## Reopening Or Supersession Effect

Reopened when reviewer role or relationship changes.

The preparer must record a concrete evidence locator for this instrument's purpose and must mark the row `INCOMPLETE` if the locator is absent.
