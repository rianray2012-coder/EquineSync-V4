## Purpose

Bounded-scope self-declaration record.

## Triggering Event

## Required Inputs

Scope; checks; failure-capability; limitations; preparer statement.

## Required Evidence

Validation report; negative-control evidence; limitation log.

## Exclusions

No independent assurance or third-party conformity claim.

## Responsible Preparer

First-party preparer.

## Required Reviewer

Governance reviewer.

## Approval Or Acknowledgement Field

Self-declaration acknowledgement only; blank until an authenticated Founder or reviewer action is supplied.

## Authority Effect

First-party statement only. This template does not authorize adoption, activation, implementation, production use, certification completion, merge, or protected-branch mutation.

## Prohibited Conclusions

Do not imply certification or external accreditation.

## Completion Criteria

All checks identify capability and limitations.

## Reopening Or Supersession Effect

Reopened when validation source or package bytes change.
