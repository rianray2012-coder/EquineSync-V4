# Maintenance Dry-Run Plan

Monthly integrity review: run generator drift check, validator, checksum verification, pointer resolution, and placeholder/overclaim scan. Estimated effort: 1-2 hours after automation setup.

Quarterly governance review: sample open certifications, waivers, findings, source gaps, lifecycle states, exception budget, regulatory questions, and role assignments. Estimated effort: 4-8 hours depending on open exceptions.

Pre-release review: full-control profile for any production, privacy, safeguarding, security, financial, AI, minors, or material authority artifact. Estimated effort: 4-12 hours plus outside review where required.

Annual portfolio recertification: review artifact inventory, lifecycle distribution, retained limitations, external-obligation changes, role assignments, retention, and unresolved source gaps. Estimated effort: 1-3 days unless automation is expanded.

Staffing gap: Founder currently fills multiple roles. Recommended reduction: use lightweight profile only for low-risk non-authoritative artifacts; retain full-control profile for material authority.
