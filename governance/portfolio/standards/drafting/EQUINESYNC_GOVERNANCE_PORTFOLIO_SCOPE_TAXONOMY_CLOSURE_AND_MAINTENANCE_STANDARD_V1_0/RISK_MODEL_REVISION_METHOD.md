# Risk Model Revision Method

Risk scoring is categorical, not false mathematical precision. Evaluate authority consequence, personal-data sensitivity, minors and safeguarding, security impact, financial impact, production impact, external-law exposure, operational dependency, reversibility, evidence quality, implementation maturity, exception count and age, concentration of authority, vendor dependence, AI involvement, scope breadth, and likelihood of overclaim.

Risk levels: `LOW`, `MODERATE`, `HIGH`, `CRITICAL`.

A single critical factor may elevate the whole artifact. Low evidence quality, production effect, minors/safeguarding, personal data, security, financial operations, or external-law exposure must not be hidden by averaging.
