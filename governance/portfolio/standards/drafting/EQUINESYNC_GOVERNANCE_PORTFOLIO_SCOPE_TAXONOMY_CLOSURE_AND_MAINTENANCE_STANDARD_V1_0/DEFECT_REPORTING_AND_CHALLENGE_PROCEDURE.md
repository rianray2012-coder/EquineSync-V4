# Defect Reporting And Challenge Procedure

Authorized employees, contractors, reviewers, advisors, customer representatives, or other authorized persons may report suspected non-falsification violations, unsupported overclaims, invalid certifications, undisclosed conflicts, privacy concerns, implementation discrepancies, or incorrect closure claims.

A credible report creates a reopening trigger under `ES-GPS-CHAL-001`. The governance steward must record the report, affected artifacts, immediate claim effect, owner, investigation evidence, disposition, and whether the affected claim is reclosed, narrowed, suspended, superseded, retired, or rejected.

No report may be closed silently. Retaliation or suppression of truthful governance defects is outside the authority of this standard.
