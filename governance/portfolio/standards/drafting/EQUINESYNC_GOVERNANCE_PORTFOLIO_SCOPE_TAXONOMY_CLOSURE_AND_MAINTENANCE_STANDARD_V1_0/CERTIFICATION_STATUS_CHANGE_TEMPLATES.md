# Certification Status Change Templates

## Revocation Record

Fields: record_id, target_certification_id, revoking_authority, effective_at, reason, dependent_claim_effect, notice, retained_evidence.

## Narrowing Record

Fields: record_id, target_certification_id, prior_scope, narrowed_scope, effective_at, reason, dependent_claim_effect.

## Suspension Record

Fields: record_id, target_certification_id, suspension_reason, effective_at, required_review, dependent_claim_effect.

## Supersession Record

Fields: record_id, predecessor_certification_id, successor_certification_id, supersession_scope, effective_at, retained_limitations.
