## Required Truth Statement

A waived, deferred, replaced, or substituted test must not be reported as passed unless it was actually executed and passed. Pilot-generated evidence may satisfy a requirement only within the scope expressly accepted by the Founder. Production authority exists only if expressly granted for an exact head, release, environment, feature scope, and exception set.
