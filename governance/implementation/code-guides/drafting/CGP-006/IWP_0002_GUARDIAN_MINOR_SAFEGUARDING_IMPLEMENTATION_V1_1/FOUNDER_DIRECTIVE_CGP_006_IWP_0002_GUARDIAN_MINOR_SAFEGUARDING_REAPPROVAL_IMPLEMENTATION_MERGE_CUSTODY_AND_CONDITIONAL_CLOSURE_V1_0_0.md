## 6. Mandatory Safety Invariants

The implementation shall enforce all V1.1.0 package invariants, including the following non-negotiable requirements:

1. Resolve canonical students and every affected minor server-side from authoritative repository data.
2. Derive age at decision time. Unknown, future, malformed, contradictory, or unverified age data fails closed for guarded workflows.
3. Require an active, same-barn Guardian relationship with authority scope covering the exact workflow.
4. Fail closed for revoked, expired, suspended, disputed, restricted, cross-barn, contradictory, or otherwise non-qualifying authority.
5. Keep Guardian relationship, authority scope, and workflow consent as separate concepts and records.
6. Require consent to be explicit, attributable, versioned, correctly scoped, auditable, revocable, and time-bounded where applicable.
7. Never manufacture, infer, or silently grandfather missing legacy consent.
8. For multi-minor communications, prove qualifying Guardian coverage independently for each minor.
9. A Guardian counted for messaging must be an actual authorized participant in the thread or transaction, not merely linked elsewhere.
10. Derive minor involvement from authoritative participants, recipients, student references, and route state. Omission of optional caller metadata must not bypass the guard.
11. Reevaluate Guardian, authority, and consent state before every guarded write.
12. Allow lawful Guardian relationship revocation or suspension even when it leaves no qualifying Guardian. Subsequent minor workflows must then fail closed.
13. Block removal of the last qualifying Guardian participant from an active minor-involved conversation unless the thread is closed or archived, or a qualifying replacement is added atomically.
14. Use a transaction, conditional version, commit-time revalidation, or equivalent repository-native consistency mechanism to prevent time-of-check/time-of-use bypass.
15. Authorization caches must be absent, request-scoped, or explicitly invalidated or version-checked when relationship, authority, consent, policy, participant, barn, or age state changes.
16. Internal audit codes may be precise, but public API responses must not disclose sensitive custody, dispute, relationship, consent, or minor-state facts to unauthorized callers.
17. Under-13 access remains parent-managed only. Supported access for ages 13 through 17 remains subject to active Guardian controls.
18. Minor payment actions default to denied unless existing approved billing authority and exact workflow consent are evidenced. No Stripe or other provider call is authorized for this workstream.
19. No implicit emergency or convenience override may be created.
20. Frontend state is explanatory only. Server-side enforcement controls.
21. Adult and valid Guardian workflows must remain functional unless another existing authorization rule independently blocks them.
22. Tenant and barn isolation must remain intact at every read, decision, write, audit, and participant-resolution boundary.
