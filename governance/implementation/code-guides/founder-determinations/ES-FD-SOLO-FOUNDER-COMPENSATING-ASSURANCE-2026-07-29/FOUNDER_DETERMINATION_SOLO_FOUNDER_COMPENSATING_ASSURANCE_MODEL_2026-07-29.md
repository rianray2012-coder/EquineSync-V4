# 1. PURPOSE

This Founder Determination establishes the assurance model appropriate to EquineSync’s present organizational, financial, development, and operational circumstances.

EquineSync is presently a solo-Founder project. The Founder is the only human actively directing product design, governance, documentary development, repository work, implementation planning, and project administration.

EquineSync does not presently have:

- employees;
- an internal engineering team;
- an internal assurance or compliance department;
- an independent technical reviewer;
- a retained software architect;
- a retained security adviser;
- a retained equine-domain consultant;
- project funds available for paid external technical, domain, or assurance review.

The absence of those resources shall not indefinitely prevent EquineSync from:

- completing its governance framework;
- activating Code Guides for appropriately limited scopes;
- performing repository-specific implementation mapping;
- completing Founder-authorized technical development;
- creating staging evidence;
- conducting a controlled web-first pilot;
- preparing a viable product for broader launch.

EquineSync shall instead use a documented solo-Founder compensating assurance model based on transparent self-review, domain-owner review, machine-assisted adversarial analysis, deterministic validation, objective testing, staged authority, controlled rollout, runtime evidence, and explicit Founder residual-risk acceptance.

This Determination does not create or imply independent human assurance.

# 5. SOLO-FOUNDER COMPENSATING ASSURANCE MODEL

The Founder approves the following model as the current assurance pathway for EquineSync governance, internal development, staging, controlled pilot preparation, and evidence generation.

## 5.1 Exact-byte source control

Every controlling guide, plan, review source, implementation profile, and approval candidate shall be:

- version identified;
- exact-byte frozen;
- SHA-256 recorded;
- manifest controlled;
- traceable to repository custody;
- reviewed against the same frozen bytes.

No review may be applied to substituted or materially changed source bytes without a new review record.

## 5.2 Conflict and non-independence disclosure

Every Founder-led or Codex-assisted review shall disclose:

- the Founder’s ownership and decision authority;
- the Founder’s role in defining product requirements;
- Codex involvement in drafting or analysis;
- absence of an independent human reviewer;
- limits on the assurance created.

No self-review or machine-assisted review may be described as independent human assurance.

## 5.3 Temporal separation

For material review work, authoring and review shall be separated by a documented review boundary.

The process shall, where reasonably practicable:

1. freeze the reviewed source;
2. close the authoring phase;
3. begin a separately identified review phase;
4. prohibit silent editing during review;
5. record findings before remediation;
6. perform remediation in a separate phase;
7. rerun review against newly frozen bytes.

Temporal separation does not create independence, but it reduces contemporaneous authoring bias and improves evidentiary clarity.

## 5.4 Founder Domain-Owner Review

The Founder shall complete a structured guide-by-guide domain-owner review before final Stage 24 activation disposition.

The review shall address:

- practical barn and facility use;
- horse-owner workflows;
- rider and guardian workflows;
- lesson-program workflows;
- trainer and staff workflows;
- minor-participant and safeguarding considerations;
- communication boundaries;
- task and care evidence;
- correction and accountability;
- realistic interruption and failure conditions;
- compatibility with intended users and product requirements.

The permitted completion statement is:

`FOUNDER_DOMAIN_OWNER_REVIEW_COMPLETE_WITH_DISCLOSED_NON_INDEPENDENCE`

Completion shall not be claimed until the structured review record exists.

## 5.5 Founder Technical Governance Review

The Founder may perform a structured technical-governance review assisted by repository evidence, deterministic validators, machine analysis, and test results.

The permitted completion statement is:

`FOUNDER_TECHNICAL_GOVERNANCE_REVIEW_COMPLETE_WITH_DISCLOSED_SELF_REVIEW`

It shall not be described as independent peer review.

## 5.6 Multi-pass machine-assisted adversarial review

Material governance and implementation packages shall receive multiple review passes with distinct assignments, including as applicable:

- architecture and authority review;
- control and invariant review;
- adversarial and misuse review;
- permission and tenant-isolation review;
- testability review;
- evidence and traceability review;
- operational and recovery review;
- implementer-usability review.

Where practicable, individual passes shall be conducted without relying on prior-pass conclusions before the pass records its own findings.

The permitted description is:

`MULTI_PASS_MACHINE_ASSISTED_ADVERSARIAL_REVIEW_COMPLETE`

It shall not be described as independent human review.

## 5.7 Objective verification

Assurance shall rely materially on reproducible evidence, including as applicable:

- deterministic validators;
- unit tests;
- integration tests;
- API contract tests;
- database-backed tests;
- migration tests;
- end-to-end browser tests;
- negative tests;
- misuse and abuse tests;
- duplicate-action and idempotency tests;
- stale-state and race-condition tests;
- dependency scanning;
- secret scanning;
- static security analysis;
- authorization tests;
- tenant and facility isolation tests;
- guardian and minor-control tests;
- upload and export tests;
- account recovery and session-revocation tests;
- backup and restoration drills;
- monitoring and alert verification;
- rollback and feature-disable verification.

A passing scanner or build alone shall not be treated as complete assurance.

## 5.8 Threat modeling

Formal threat modeling shall be performed for high-risk areas, including as applicable:

- identity and authentication;
- tenancy and facility isolation;
- authorization and permissions;
- guardians and minors;
- horse health and care records;
- payments;
- documents and signatures;
- file uploads;
- administrative and support access;
- offline synchronization;
- integrations and providers;
- AI and automation.

Each material threat shall identify:

- affected asset;
- threat or failure scenario;
- severity;
- preventive control;
- detection control;
- recovery control;
- verification method;
- evidence location;
- residual-risk treatment.

## 5.9 Controlled implementation

Implementation shall proceed only through separately authorized, bounded work packages.

Each authorized implementation package shall identify:

- governing guides;
- repository surfaces;
- permitted changes;
- prohibited changes;
- required tests;
- security and privacy controls;
- rollback;
- evidence requirements;
- stopping conditions;
- Founder acceptance boundary.

## 5.10 Controlled staging and pilot

A controlled pilot shall be used as an assurance mechanism before broad public launch.

Unless a later Founder directive provides otherwise, the initial pilot posture shall be:

`ONE_FACILITY`

`INVITE_ONLY`

`FOUNDER_OBSERVED`

`WEB_FIRST`

`ONLINE_FIRST`

`LIMITED_FIELD_RECOVERY`

`NO_PUBLIC_REGISTRATION`

`NO_PLATFORM_CUSTODY_OF_CLIENT_FUNDS`

`NO_DIRECT_UNDER_13_ACCOUNTS`

`NO_AUTONOMOUS_MEDICAL_OR_SAFETY_DECISIONS`

`NO_UNVERIFIED_PROVIDER_LIVE_CLAIMS`

`FEATURE_FLAGS_REQUIRED`

`ROLLBACK_AVAILABLE`

Pilot participation, staging deployment, and runtime evidence collection remain subject to separate Founder authorization.

## 5.11 Founder residual-risk acceptance

Before staging, pilot, or launch, the Founder shall receive:

- current findings;
- failed or deferred controls;
- residual risks;
- unavailable evidence;
- accepted limitations;
- rollback and containment options;
- recommended disposition.

Residual risk shall not be accepted by silence.

# 6. REVIEW-GATE RECLASSIFICATION

## 6.1 Independent technical review

The absence of independent human technical review is reclassified as:

`DISCLOSED_NON_INDEPENDENT_ASSURANCE_LIMITATION`

It shall remain truthfully recorded.

It does not, by itself, block:

- completion of the Code Guide governance program;
- limited documentary guide activation;
- repository-specific implementation mapping;
- Founder-authorized internal implementation;
- staging;
- controlled web-first pilot preparation;
- controlled web-first pilot operation;
- public web-launch consideration after all applicable compensating assurance gates pass.

It continues to block:

- any claim of independent human technical review;
- any claim of third-party technical certification;
- any claim that EquineSync received outside professional assurance;
- any public representation that the project was independently audited when it was not.

Independent review may be obtained later if the Founder determines it is necessary or resources become available. It is not established by this Determination as an automatic prerequisite to the current governance, build, staging, pilot, or web-launch path.

## 6.2 Human domain-expert review

The requirement for direct human domain review shall be satisfied for the current project stage through a completed and protectedly recorded Founder Domain-Owner Review.

The review must state:

`FOUNDER_DOMAIN_OWNER_REVIEW_COMPLETE_WITH_DISCLOSED_NON_INDEPENDENCE`

Until that review record is complete, no completion claim may be made.

Outside equine-domain review is not required for the present governance, implementation, staging, or controlled pilot path.

## 6.3 Implementer-usability review

Where an independent implementer is unavailable, implementer-usability review may be performed through:

- a clean-room planning exercise;
- temporally separated review;
- isolated Codex planning passes;
- explicit recording of every material guess;
- Founder evaluation of whether the guide requires invention of product policy;
- later validation against actual implementation experience.

The result shall not be described as an independent human implementer review.

## 6.4 Implementation and runtime evidence

Implementation evidence remains required after authorized implementation exists.

Runtime evidence remains required after an authorized staging, pilot, or operational environment exists.

Neither evidence class shall be claimed before it exists.

Neither shall be used as a circular prerequisite that permanently prohibits the authority needed to create the evidence.
