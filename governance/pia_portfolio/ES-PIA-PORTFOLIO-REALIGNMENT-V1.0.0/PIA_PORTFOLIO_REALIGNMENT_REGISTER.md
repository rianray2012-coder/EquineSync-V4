## Binding placement rules

1. Enrollment is contained within Item 01 and cannot be an eleventh PIA.
2. Relationships and Delegated Authority is Item 03 Component A.
3. Authorization and Permission are Item 03 Components B and C.
4. Stage 2A material is a MIAP cross-cutting profile, never a PIA.
5. Canons are sources; ADRs and contracts are subordinate; review/remediation/handoff packages create no portfolio positions.
6. The current successor Identity and Relationships text is pending fresh segregated review and Founder ratification. Only the earlier underlying designs retain their recorded design-approval status.
7. Splits, mergers, additions, and controlling renames require a separate Founder decision.
