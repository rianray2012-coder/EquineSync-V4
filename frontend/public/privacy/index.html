EquineSync Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

EquineSync provides software for horse owners, trainers, barn managers, facility owners, and related equine-care participants. This Privacy Policy explains how EquineSync collects, uses, and protects information when you use EquineSync websites, applications, communications, and services.

Information We Collect

We may collect account information, contact information, role and facility information, horse and care-management records entered by users, service preferences, payment or subscription status from payment processors, support requests, device and usage data, and communication preferences.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide EquineSync services, manage accounts and subscriptions, support barn and horse-care workflows, send service communications, respond to support requests, maintain security, improve product reliability, and comply with legal and operational obligations.

SMS Privacy And Consent

If you choose to opt in to EquineSync SMS messages, we use your mobile phone number to send customer care and urgent operational text messages related to your EquineSync account, barn coordination, schedule or task reminders, invite or account setup reminders, support follow-up, and guardian or parent notices when applicable.

SMS consent is optional and is not required to create an account or use EquineSync. Message frequency varies based on account activity and urgent operational needs. Message and data rates may apply. You may reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

EquineSync does not share SMS opt-in consent or mobile phone numbers with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Information Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help operate EquineSync, such as hosting, storage, payment, email, messaging, analytics, monitoring, support, and document services. These providers are used to deliver and secure EquineSync services. We do not sell personal information.

Data Protection

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No system can guarantee absolute security, but EquineSync works to limit access and preserve privacy-sensitive details in external communications.

Your Choices

You may update communication preferences in EquineSync where available. For SMS messages, reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help. You may also contact EquineSync through the website at https://app.equine-sync.com/.

Terms Of Service

The EquineSync Terms of Service are available at https://app.equine-sync.com/terms.
