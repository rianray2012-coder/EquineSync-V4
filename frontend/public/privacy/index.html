EquineSync

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

EquineSync helps horse owners, trainers, barn teams, and equine service providers coordinate care records, scheduling, barn operations, payments, documents, and urgent communications.

Information We Collect

We may collect account information, contact details, role and facility membership information, horse care records, scheduling details, billing status, uploaded files, support requests, device and log data, and communication preferences needed to operate EquineSync.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide EquineSync services, authenticate users, route role-appropriate access, send requested notifications, support billing and account management, improve reliability, prevent misuse, and comply with legal or operational obligations.

SMS And Mobile Information

EquineSync SMS messages are used for urgent account, barn operations, scheduling, and safety-related notices after a user opts in. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to opt out.

EquineSync does not sell, rent, or share mobile opt-in information, phone numbers, or SMS consent data with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties.

Service Providers

We may use service providers for hosting, email, storage, payments, messaging, analytics, monitoring, document workflows, and customer support. These providers are used to operate EquineSync and are not authorized to use EquineSync data for their own marketing.

Choices And Contact

Users can manage account preferences in EquineSync where available, use STOP to opt out of SMS, or contact support for privacy requests.
