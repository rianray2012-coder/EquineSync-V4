Keyword Opt-In Reply

Supported opt-in keywords: START, YES, UNSTOP.

EquineSync: You are opted in to EquineSync Urgent Alerts. Msg freq varies. Msg and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out.

Message Scope

SMS is reserved for urgent pilot alerts such as safety-sensitive barn notices, time-sensitive schedule changes, account access issues, critical care coordination, and service-provider arrival or delay updates. SMS messages will not include detailed horse medical information on the lock screen or in message preview text.
